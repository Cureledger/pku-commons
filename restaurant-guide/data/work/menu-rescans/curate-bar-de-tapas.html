From the Kitchen

Chef-Founder Katie Button and Executive Chef Ángel Palacios

The Cúrate menu is rooted in the lively tapas culture of Spain, inspired by its prized jamón Ibérico, vermuterías, and spirit of sobremesa. Guided by Spanish tradition and local Appalachian ingredients, we believe in the curative power of gathering over good food and wine. This approach has earned national and international recognition, influenced by our continued focus on creating meaningful experiences through genuine hospitality and quality ingredients.

Our Wine Menu

by Wine Director Jessica Salyer

Our all-Spanish wine list reflects years of travel, study, and relationships cultivated by Félix Meana and Wine Director Jessica Salyer. Recognized nationally by Wine Enthusiast and Food & Wine, our selection spans Spain’s diverse winemaking regions, from the Canary Islands to Rioja, highlighting small producers, distinctive vineyards, and an extensive selection of Sherry and fortified wines.
