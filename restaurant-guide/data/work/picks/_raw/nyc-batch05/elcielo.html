Juan Manuel Barrientos
Executive Chef & Owner

Global Creative and Culinary Direction

Colombian chef, entrepreneur, and founder of Elcielo Hospitality Group. Juanma has brought Colombian gastronomy to international stages through innovation, technique, and sensory-driven experiences. Trained by renowned chefs such as Juan Mari Arzak and Iwao Komiyama, his culinary approach is rooted in research, creativity, and deep respect for ingredients.

He leads Elcielo’s global culinary vision and the development of concepts now present in Medellín, Bogotá, Miami, Washington D.C., and New York. His work has been recognized by the MICHELIN Guide, which has awarded stars to Elcielo Miami and Elcielo Washington D.C., and a MICHELIN Key to Elcielo Hotel Medellín.

He oversees the group’s strategic expansion, sensory innovation, and social impact initiatives through Elcielo Foundation.

Sebastian Moreno Henao
Executive Sous Chef & Corporate Chef USA, Elcielo Hospitality Group 

Culinary coordination and standards.

Sebastián Moreno Henao is a Colombian chef with more than fifteen years of experience in haute cuisine and the hospitality industry, and one of the foundational figures of Elcielo Hospitality Group, where he has worked since 2012 and has been part of the United States operations since 2015, serving as Corporate Chef USA since 2022. Currently Executive Sous Chef and Corporate Chef USA, he has overseen the culinary direction of Elcielo’s Michelin-starred restaurants in Miami and Washington, D.C., and is now leading the opening and development of Elcielo New York. Renowned for his commitment to elevating Colombian cuisine on the global stage through sensory-driven fine dining, Sebastián has been instrumental in shaping the group’s tasting menus, R&D programs, and culinary innovation, contributing brand’s development. Since 2020, he has supported the leadership of the first Colombian restaurant in history to earn a Michelin Star—Elcielo Washington D.C.—recognized consecutively from 2021 through 2025. His expertise spans recipe and menu development, culinary leadership, GMP compliance, and guiding the standards and creative direction of the group’s restaurant kitchens across multiple cities. Sebastián’s international background includes stages at world-renowned institutions such as El Celler de Can Roca (3 Michelin Stars, Girona), Restaurant Bror (Copenhagen), Nordic Food Lab, and Noma (3 Michelin Stars, Copenhagen) and BIKO restaurant (Mexico City).

Juan David Aristizábal Daza
Chef de Cuisine, Elcielo New York

Kitchen leadership

Juan David Aristizábal Daza is a Colombian chef with more than a decade of experience in haute cuisine, recognized for his ability to conceptualize, develop, and execute gastronomic projects from ideation to full operation.

A member of Elcielo Hospitality Group since 2019, he currently leads the kitchen at Elcielo New York as Chef de Cuisine, bringing to the city a contemporary interpretation of Colombian fine dining rooted in technique, creativity, and cultural depth. His career includes formative stages and leadership roles in acclaimed international restaurants such as El Celler de Can Roca in Girona (3 Michelin Stars), Clooney in Auckland (awarded 3 Hats by the Good Food Guide), Restaurante Ambar, and Bistronomy by Rausch, experiences that strengthened his precision, flavor development, and avant-garde culinary perspective.

Juan David holds a Master’s Degree in Advanced Culinary Techniques from the Basque Culinary Center in San Sebastián, a professional culinary degree from Gato Dumas in Bogotá, and additional training in restaurant and bar design from the Universidad Pontificia Bolivariana, as well as vacuum-cooking techniques from the Michelin-starred restaurant Etxanobe.
