TEA PAVILION

 

A Reflection of Korean Heritage & Hospitality

An oasis full of discovery and relaxation, the Tea Pavilion invites guests to experience the deeply-rooted traditions and beauty of Korean tea culture. Immerse yourself in the modern interpretation of a traditional Korean hanok and discover the art of ancient tea rituals.

Explore the distinctly Korean experience.

 

 

A Seonbi’s Sanctuary

Celebrate the simplistic elegance of classic Korean architecture amongst the serene panoramic views of the Hudson River. Embody the spirit of the ancient Korean seonbi and discover the unique selection of fine loose-leaf teas and curated literature.

 

 

Genesis House Tea Ceremony

Inspired by traditional Korean tea ceremony dating back over 1,300 years, guests will be guided through the unique rituals that create a one of kind tea experience.
