GENESIS HOUSE RESTAURANT

DISTINCTLY KOREAN CUISINE

A Culinary Expression of Korea

Inspired by centuries old recipes, Genesis House Restaurant’s living menu is a harmony of surprising flavors featuring fresh and locally grown ingredients. Crafted with traditional techniques, each dish is a modern interpretation of traditional Korean cuisine.

Reservations

Join us for a culinary exploration into the heart of Seoul where each meal is masterfully created to not only satiate the appetite, but to stimulate all the senses.

Restaurant reservations are available for both lunch and dinner. From a relaxed daytime meal to an intimate evening gathering, we would be honored to host you to create a memorable dining experience.

Terrace Garden

Surrounded by a traditional Korean landscape of white sand and gravel adorned with greenery, the expansive outdoor terrace offers beautiful views of the High Line, Hudson River, and Lower Manhattan skyline. Enjoy a beverage or light bite from the restaurant’s menu and relax in sophistication with the city as your backdrop.

Manifesting Marilyn Menu

Marilyn's story continues at the table. To mark Marilyn Monroe's centennial, the Genesis House restaurant has composed a special menu: a contemporary interpretation of traditional Korean cuisine, inspired by the icon herself: 'The Centennial,' her favorite champagne with soju, finished ruby red; 'Before the Flash,' a zero-proof pour of chamoe shrub, rice vinegar, and club soda; 'The Rewrite,' a chilled tangpyung-chae of mungbean jelly, squid, and scallop; and 'Greenlit,' a pistachio cheesecake sealed in green, the color of her dreams.

Available through August 2.
