Nina named the factors on 2026-08-23. Each filled dot is one yes. Empty is not-yet, not a zero.

1. Published menu
2. A main
3. Plates beyond potato and salad
4. Accommodation — they will substitute
5. Off-menu — they will cook off-menu
6. A third-party award
7. Phebe-verified

Mains and plates count every filed PKU option. Potato and salad are plates. The third dot fills only when there is at least one plate that is not potato or salad.

Accommodation, off-menu, and Phebe-verified stay unset until contact or a visit. Do not infer them from menu text.
